Parts that click into place: onboarding motion for the machine parts app

Textile's onboarding moves like cloth: slow folds and words rising. This app moves like a workshop. Parts glide along axis lines and seat with a click, bolts ratchet tight, carriages run on rails, and gauges read. Every drawing tells that screen's story, and the left/right matching idea opens the app.

06 · GST

A caliper reads the GSTIN

A vernier caliper measures the 15 characters part by part: state, PAN, entity, the fixed Z and the check digit. A readout names each part. Once all five pass, the full span is "within tolerance" and the registered details slide up.

  1. per partJaws open to the part (750 ms rail move); its characters light up, then underline green.
  2. after 5Jaws open to the full 15 characters; "within tolerance" seats.
  3. +0.25 sThe GST card rises with Matches your profile.

Building it in the app

  • Same layered approach as textile's onboarding: each moving part is its own full-size Svg in an Animated.View, driven by one 0–1 loop value with keyframe tracks; the percentages here are the tracks.
  • Glide, Seat, Rail and Ratchet become four named easings in lib/motion; ratchet is a stepped interpolation.
  • Mobile number, Code and GST react to real input (digits typed, the GSTIN parts), so they are state-driven, not loops.
  • Reduce motion shows every drawing at its rest frame (assembled hub, tightened bolts, engraved plate, landed pin, measured GSTIN). Switch it on above to see them.
  • No new dependency; no fabric folds, rising words or bouncing.
