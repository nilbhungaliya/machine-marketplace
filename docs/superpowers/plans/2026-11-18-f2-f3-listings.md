**Goal:** A business lists a spare part, a machine for sale or (GST-verified only) a machine for rent through an
autosaved wizard with photos and up to two 60-second videos; listings pass one go-live gate (review for unverified
sellers, a verified seller's first 3, and unaccepted sanity flags), live on a public listing page with Fits, Other side
and seller blocks, move through one server-side state machine (pause, sold, rented, expiry after 60 days, renew,
relist, remove), and are moderated from the admin panel (queue, review, hide / remove, reports, all listings,
Marketplace settings). Left / right parts find their other side.

**Architecture:** New Drizzle tables (`listings` + 1 : 1 `listing_machines` / `listing_rentals`, `listing_fitments`,
`listing_media`, `listing_status_history`, generic `reports`, `app_settings`) in `apps/api/src/db/schema.ts`. All rules
in `apps/api/src/services/listings/*.service.ts`: **`listing-lifecycle.service.ts` is the only writer of `status`**

`other-side.service.ts` the matching query; `listing-admin.service.ts` moderation. The transition table, sanity flags,
score, part-number and part-name normalisers, settings registry and `LISTING_VIDEO_UPLOAD_ENABLED` are in
`packages/shared-types`, shared by API, app and admin. Admin pages in `apps/admin` on the v2 kit; app screens in
`apps/user-app` on the v2 components.

## Review Focus

1. **A listing reaches `live` without the gate** — resume, renew, relist or end of rental after an edit that raised a
   new flag; a rental resumed after GST was revoked; relist from `sold` past the unverified cap; two concurrent
   publishes of one unverified business at cap − 1 (exactly one wins, the other `409 UNVERIFIED_LISTING_CAP`); approve
   or unhide of a listing without a photo. (Tasks 8, 9, 16.)
2. **Privacy leaks** — a guest, another business or an expired token reading a draft / paused / hidden / removed /
   rejected listing (`404`), a serial number, a godown address line, a pincode or a point in any public payload or
   Other-side card; a restricted business's listings served publicly. (Tasks 10, 14.)
3. **Media that shouldn't be public** — a key for another business, listing or `uploadId`; HTML / HEIC / AVIF bytes; a
   JPEG with an EXIF segment; a disallowed `ftyp` brand; two attaches racing past 10 photos (one `409 MEDIA_LIMIT`,
   its public copies deleted); video while the switch is off; a deleted photo still served (purge called with the
   exact URLs). Without keys every media endpoint is `503 STORAGE_UNAVAILABLE`. (Tasks 11–13, 16.)
4. **Grandfathering vs. bypass** — a non-draft edit of an unrelated field passes on a listing whose category, brand,
   unit or side setting changed since; but a changed specs object meets today's template, a changed side meets today's
   setting, category can't change after review (`409 CATEGORY_LOCKED`), and a side / fit edit on a live listing with an
   open report goes back to review. (Task 7.)
5. **Stale moderator decisions** — approve / ask for changes / reject / admin edit after the seller edited: `409
   LISTING_CHANGED`, no status change, no audit row; hide / remove / media delete apply regardless; deleting the last
   photo moves a public-side listing to `rejected`. (Task 16.)
6. **Deadlocks and races** — the expiry job finishes while another transaction holds the business lock; a renew that
   commits first wins over the job; concurrent seller actions resolve to one winner. (Tasks 6, 9, 15.)
7. **Video location metadata** — GPS-tagged host fixtures come out of the compressor without location tags, or video
   stays off (and the API refuses it). (Tasks 13, 20.)
